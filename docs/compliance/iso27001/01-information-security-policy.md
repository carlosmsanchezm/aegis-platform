## 1. Management Commitment

Aegis Technologies is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets. This policy establishes the foundation of our Information Security Management System (ISMS) in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

As the founder and top management of Aegis Technologies, I commit to:

- Providing the resources necessary to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the ISMS
- Ensuring information security objectives are established and compatible with the strategic direction of the company
- Ensuring ISMS requirements are integrated into our business processes
- Promoting continual improvement
- Supporting other relevant management roles to demonstrate their leadership

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Information Security Objectives

Aegis Technologies shall:

1. **Protect customer trust** by securing the software development lifecycle
2. **Maintain compliance** with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
3. **Enable secure product delivery** without impeding development velocity
4. **Manage risks** to an acceptable level through systematic assessment and treatment
5. **Continuously improve** the effectiveness of the ISMS

### 4.2 Security Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Confidentiality** | Protect information from unauthorized disclosure |
| **Integrity** | Protect information from unauthorized modification |
| **Availability** | Ensure authorized access when needed |
| **Accountability** | Maintain audit trails and clear responsibilities |
| **Defense in Depth** | Implement multiple layers of security controls |
| **Least Privilege** | Grant minimum access necessary |
| **Security by Design** | Build security into products from inception |

### 4.3 Risk Management

The organization shall:
- Identify and assess information security risks systematically
- Treat risks according to documented risk acceptance criteria
- Monitor and review the risk environment regularly
- Maintain a risk register and treatment plan

### 4.4 Compliance

The organization shall:
- Identify applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
- Ensure compliance with identified requirements
- Monitor for changes in compliance obligations
- Maintain certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001)

### 4.5 Continual Improvement

The organization shall:
- Conduct regular internal audits
- Perform management reviews
- Address nonconformities and implement corrective actions
- Measure and evaluate ISMS performance

## 7. Policy Enforcement

### 7.1 Compliance Monitoring

Compliance with this policy and supporting policies is monitored through:
- Internal audits (annual minimum)
- Management reviews (annual minimum)
- Continuous monitoring and logging
- Security assessments

### 7.2 Violations

Violations of information security policies may result in:
- Corrective action and retraining
- Disciplinary action (up to termination for employees)
- Contract termination (for contractors/vendors)
- Legal action where appropriate

### 7.3 Exceptions

Exceptions to security policies require:
- Written business justification
- Risk assessment
- Compensating controls
- Approval by Information Security Manager
- Time-limited duration
- Documentation in risk register
