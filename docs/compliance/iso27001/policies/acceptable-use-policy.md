## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Aegis Technologies' information assets to protect the organization, its employees, and its customers from security risks.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All personnel (employees, contractors, consultants)
- All information assets owned or managed by Aegis Technologies
- All systems used for Aegis business purposes

---

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 General Principles

Users shall:
- Use company resources primarily for business purposes
- Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies
- Report security incidents and policy violations promptly

Users shall NOT:
- Use resources for illegal activities
- Attempt to bypass security controls
- Share credentials or access with unauthorized persons
- Install unauthorized software on company systems

### 3.2 Acceptable Use

#### 3.2.1 Computing Resources
- ✅ Business-related development and operations
- ✅ Professional development and training
- ✅ Reasonable personal use that does not interfere with work
- ❌ Running personal businesses
- ❌ Cryptocurrency mining
- ❌ Excessive bandwidth consumption for non-work purposes

#### 3.2.2 Email and Communications
- ✅ Business communications
- ✅ Professional networking
- ❌ Spam or bulk unsolicited messages
- ❌ Harassment or offensive content
- ❌ Forwarding confidential information externally without authorization

#### 3.2.3 Internet Access
- ✅ Research and professional development
- ✅ Business-related browsing
- ❌ Accessing illegal content
- ❌ Downloading pirated software
- ❌ Visiting malicious websites

#### 3.2.4 Source Code and Development
- ✅ Company development work
- ✅ Open source contributions (with approval)
- ❌ Copying proprietary code to personal accounts
- ❌ Introducing intentional vulnerabilities
- ❌ Bypassing code review processes

### 3.3 Personal Devices (BYOD)

If personal devices are used for work:
- Full disk encryption must be enabled
- Screen lock must be configured (max 5 min timeout)
- Device must have current OS and security updates
- Company data must be removable upon request
- Lost/stolen devices must be reported immediately

### 3.4 Remote Working

When working remotely:
- Use secure (encrypted) network connections
- Do not work on sensitive data in public places where screens are visible
- Secure physical workspace from unauthorized access
- Do not use public computers for accessing company systems

### 3.5 Password and Authentication

Users must:
- Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts
- Enable MFA on all accounts where available
- Never share passwords or authentication tokens
- Use password manager for credential storage
- Report suspected credential compromise immediately

### 3.6 Data Handling

- Handle data according to its classification (Confidential, Internal, Public)
- Do not store confidential data on personal devices without encryption
- Do not transmit confidential data over unencrypted channels
- Delete data when no longer needed per retention policy

### 3.7 Social Media

- Do not disclose confidential company information
- Clearly distinguish personal opinions from company positions
- Do not make security-related disclosures without approval
- Report suspected social engineering attempts

---

## 4. Monitoring

Aegis Technologies reserves the right to monitor:
- Network traffic and system logs
- Email and communications on company systems
- Access to company resources

Monitoring is conducted for security purposes and in compliance with applicable laws.

---

## 5. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Warning and retraining
- Temporary suspension of access
- Termination of employment/contract
- Legal action where appropriate

---

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require:
- Written request with business justification
- Risk assessment
- Approval by Information Security Manager
- Time-limited duration
- Documented compensating controls
