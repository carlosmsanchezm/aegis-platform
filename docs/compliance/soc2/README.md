# SOC 2 Type II Certification

## Overview

SOC 2 Type II is an audit of Aegis's company security practices over a period of time (typically 6-12 months). This certification proves to customers that our development processes, support systems, and corporate IT meet industry security standards.

## Why SOC 2?

| Customer Type | Requirement Level | Reason |
|--------------|-------------------|--------|
| Enterprise | Often Required | Procurement requirement |
| Government (Model B) | Recommended | Trust signal |
| Government (Model C) | Required | You operate in their account |

## Trust Services Criteria (TSC)

We are targeting the following TSC categories:

- ✅ **Security** (Required) - Protection against unauthorized access
- ✅ **Availability** (For Model C) - System operational and usable
- ⬜ **Confidentiality** (If handling customer data) - Protection of confidential information

See [trust-services-criteria.md](./trust-services-criteria.md) for detailed mapping.

## Evidence Collection

Evidence must be collected continuously throughout the audit period. Key evidence types:

- Access reviews (quarterly)
- Change management records
- Security training completion
- Vulnerability scans
- Penetration test results
- Incident response logs
- System monitoring alerts

See [evidence-collection-guide.md](./evidence/evidence-collection-guide.md) for details.

## Auditor Selection Checklist

When selecting a SOC 2 auditor:

- [ ] Licensed CPA firm
- [ ] Experience with SaaS/cloud companies
- [ ] Experience with your tech stack (Kubernetes, cloud providers)
- [ ] Clear communication and timeline
- [ ] References from similar companies
- [ ] Reasonable pricing
