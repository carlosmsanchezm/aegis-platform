## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the information security program for Aegis Technologies ("the Company") to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to Company systems
- All information systems owned or operated by the Company
- All data processed, stored, or transmitted by Company systems

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Security Governance

- The Company shall maintain an information security program appropriate to its size and complexity
- Executive management shall provide oversight and support for the security program
- Security responsibilities shall be clearly defined and communicated
- The security program shall be reviewed annually

### 3.2 Risk Management

- The Company shall conduct annual risk assessments
- Identified risks shall be documented, evaluated, and treated
- Risk treatment decisions shall be approved by management
- Risk status shall be reported to executive management quarterly

### 3.3 Access Control

- Access to information systems shall be granted on a need-to-know basis
- User access shall be reviewed quarterly
- Privileged access shall be limited and monitored
- Access shall be removed promptly upon termination or role change

### 3.4 Data Protection

- Data shall be classified according to sensitivity
- Encryption shall be used for data in transit and at rest where appropriate
- Personal and sensitive data shall be handled according to applicable regulations
- Data retention and disposal shall follow documented procedures

### 3.5 System Security

- Systems shall be configured according to security baselines
- Security patches shall be applied in a timely manner
- Anti-malware controls shall be implemented on all endpoints
- Systems shall be monitored for security events

### 3.6 Incident Response

- Security incidents shall be reported immediately
- Incidents shall be investigated and documented
- Lessons learned shall be incorporated into security improvements
- Customers shall be notified of incidents affecting their data

### 3.7 Business Continuity

- Critical systems shall have documented recovery procedures
- Backups shall be performed and tested regularly
- Recovery procedures shall be tested annually
- Business continuity plans shall be maintained

### 3.8 Third-Party Security

- Third parties with access to Company data shall be assessed for security
- Contracts shall include appropriate security requirements
- Third-party access shall be monitored and reviewed
- Third-party relationships shall be reassessed annually

## 5. Compliance

### 5.1 Monitoring

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through:
- Security assessments and audits
- Access reviews
- System monitoring
- Incident analysis

### 5.2 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require:
- Business justification
- Risk assessment
- Compensating controls
- Management approval
- Time-limited duration

### 5.3 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action up to termination
- Access revocation
- Legal action where appropriate
